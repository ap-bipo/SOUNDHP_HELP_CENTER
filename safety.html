Trust & Community Safety

Safety Center

At Noisy, we are building a voice-first sanctuary for genuine connection. Your security, emotional wellbeing, and voice privacy guide every feature we create.

Our Core Safety Principles

How Noisy protects your voice and interactions by design

lock

Mutual Connections Only

Noisy has no public stranger feed or unsolicited direct messages. Voice notes and conversations can only ever be shared between users who have mutually agreed to connect.

graphic_eq

Voice Privacy by Default

You have granular control over who hears your voice. Before posting an audio note, you can select whether it goes to all mutual connections or only specific close friends.

shield

Zero Tolerance for Abuse

We do not tolerate harassment, hate speech, bullying, impersonation, or non-consensual voice sharing. Violating accounts are suspended and permanently banned.

In-App Safety Tools

Simple, effective controls to manage your safety and comfort at any time

block How to Block a User

Blocking a user immediately breaks any connection, hides your profile, stops them from hearing your voice notes, and prevents them from messaging you:

1

Open the User's Profile

Navigate to the profile of the user you want to block (via search or your connection list).

2

Tap More Options

Tap the three dots (•••) icon located at the top right of their profile.

3

Confirm Block

Select Block User and confirm. The user will not be notified that they were blocked.

flag How to Report Harmful Content

If you encounter content or behavior that violates our Community Guidelines, you can submit an urgent report to our moderation team:

1

Locate the Note or Message

Tap the options menu on the offending voice post card or long-press the chat message.

2

Select Report

Choose the relevant violation category (Harassment, Inappropriate Content, Spam, or Impersonation).

3

Moderation Review

Our team reviews reports within 24 hours. If verified, the content is permanently deleted and the offending account may be terminated.

Community Guidelines for Voice

The rules that keep Noisy a supportive and authentic environment

check_circle What We Encourage

  • Sharing genuine daily thoughts and friendly banter.
  • Cheering on your close friends through voice notes.
  • Practicing mutual respect and positive communication.
  • Respecting other people's privacy and audio boundaries.

cancel What Is Strictly Prohibited

  • Bullying, intimidation, harassment, or verbal threats.
  • Sharing private voice recordings outside of Noisy without consent.
  • Hate speech targeting race, religion, gender, or sexual orientation.
  • Promoting self-harm, illegal activities, or explicit adult content.
