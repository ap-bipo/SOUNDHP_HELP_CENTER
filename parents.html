Everything you need to know about Noisy: how our audio-first platform works, how we protect your teen's privacy, and practical conversation starters for families.

What is Noisy?

Noisy was designed to bring human authenticity back to digital social life. Instead of endless text feeds, performative photo filters, and addicting algorithms, Noisy lets users exchange short (under 60-second) voice notes with their real friends.

Hearing a friend's real laughter, excitement, and voice nuance creates genuine emotional empathy. Most importantly, Noisy is built around closed mutual connections—meaning your teen is talking to friends they know, rather than broadcasting to strangers on the open internet.

Built-In Protections for Teens

How we safeguard teens from online hazards

person_pin

No Public Discovery Feed

There is no public algorithmic explore feed. Strangers cannot stumble upon your teen's voice recordings or send unprompted audio messages.

location_off

Zero Location Tracking

Noisy never asks for or tracks your teen's GPS location. Their whereabouts are completely private and never shared with anyone.

phonelink_erase

Instant Account Purge

Teens and parents have the absolute right to delete all data at any moment. Account deletion completely purges all audio files from our storage.

Tips & Conversation Starters

Questions you can ask your teen to encourage safe and healthy digital voice habits

chat_bubble_outline

"Who are you connected with?"

Encourage your teen to only accept connection requests from classmates and people they actually know in real life. Remind them that they never have to accept an invite.

record_voice_over

"Voice Tone & Digital Empathy"

Talk about how voice notes can convey warmth, but can also be hurtful if used sarcastically. Remind them not to say anything in an audio note that they wouldn't say in person.

block

"Know how to block and report"

Ensure your teen knows how to use the in-app Block and Report buttons if anyone ever makes them feel uncomfortable, pressured, or unsafe.

schedule

"Balance and Boundaries"

Establish clear screen-time routines during homework and bedtime. Remind teens that audio notifications don't require an immediate reply.

Frequently Asked Questions by Parents

Direct answers regarding parental supervision and account controls

Can strangers contact or listen to my teen on Noisy?

add
No. Noisy is designed around mutual connections. Users can only send voice notes or direct messages to accounts they have mutually connected with. There is no open public audio feed.

Does Noisy collect my child's location or sell their data?

add
No. We do not collect GPS location, and we never sell user data or audio recordings to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

How can a parent request account deletion for their teen?

add
Parents or guardians can email our team directly at soundhp.team@gmail.com with the subject "Parent Account Deletion Request" and provide the teen's registered email or @handle. We will promptly delete the account and all associated voice data.

What happens to audio recordings when an account is deleted?

add
All audio files are permanently and irreversibly removed from our cloud storage buckets. We do not maintain backups of deleted voice recordings.
